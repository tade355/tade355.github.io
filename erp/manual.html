How the ERP fits together

Record the source activity once, link it to the correct project and date, then follow it through review, settlement and reporting.

01 · RECORDWhat happened?Work, machine status, supply, cost or attendance.
02 · REVIEWWho must decide?The authorised approver checks the request and evidence.
03 · FOLLOW THROUGHWhat was issued or paid?Record fuel handouts, actual payments and outstanding balances.
04 · RECONCILEDo the records agree?Review stock, liabilities, reports and profitability.

A practical daily routine

  1. At the start: staff clock in. The site manager confirms the project’s machine status and checks available fuel.
  2. When fuel arrives: log the station collection or delivery with its project, date, fuel type and quantity. Record each site handout to a dozer.
  3. At close of work: submit operation hours, output and fuel figures. Count diesel physically left on site, and verify unusual dozer tank figures.
  4. For costs: request funds before a new spend. If the cost already happened, use Earlier Cost / Reimbursement Request and its actual incurred date. Review the manager’s accumulated balance and part payments.
  5. For control: review the Daily Operations Dashboard, Daily Reconciliation, approvals and Action Center. Resolve missing or inconsistent records.
  6. For salaries: HR prepares payroll; the authorised approver reviews it; Accounts records each payment. Staff check My Pay & Allowances.
