Day & night

Earth spins on its axis roughly once every 24 hours. Half the globe always faces the Sun (day) while the other half faces away (night). The line between them is the terminator.

Sunrise & sunset

As your location spins into the lit half you get sunrise; as it spins out, sunset. How long daylight lasts depends on your latitude and the season — try dragging both sliders.

Moon phases

The Moon doesn't change shape — we just see different amounts of its sunlit half as it orbits Earth roughly every 27.3 days, changing the Sun–Earth–Moon angle.

Seasons

Earth's axis stays tilted 23.4° in the same direction all year. As Earth orbits, that tilt points the Northern or Southern Hemisphere toward the Sun — that's what makes summer and winter, not distance from the Sun.
